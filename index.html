FinTech · NLP

Pesa Salama Classifier

A real-time financial complaint monitor and distress index for Kenya's mobile money ecosystem. CASE 02

H1N1 Vaccine Uptake Prediction

A binary classification project predicting H1N1 vaccine uptake from the 2009 H1N1 Flu Survey, using demographic, behavioral, and health-belief features. CASE 03
Customer Analytics
In Progress

SmartTel Retention Intelligence

A decision-intelligence platform for telecom churn, built on the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset (7,043 customers). Rather than stopping at a churn probability, the system is scoped to answer four operational questions: who is worth intervening on, when they're likely to leave, why they're at risk, and whether saving them is profitable — turning churn scoring into a budget-constrained, ROI-optimized retention decision.

CASE 04
Recommender Systems

Movie Recommendation System

A hybrid recommender built on the MovieLens dataset, combining collaborative filtering (tuned SVD matrix factorization) with content-based filtering (TF-IDF over genres and tags). The hybrid blend — weighted by an alpha parameter — balances personalization against content similarity to address the cold-start problem for new users and new titles.
